Frequently Asked Questions

Do you accommodate dietary restrictions?

Yes! We can accommodate vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, and other dietary needs. Please mention these when making your reservation.

Can we host private events at Hot Grill?

Absolutely! We have private dining spaces available. Contact our events team at catering@hotgrill.com for details.

Do you offer takeout or delivery?

We primarily focus on dine-in experiences to ensure the highest quality. Call us to discuss special arrangements.

What is your cancellation policy?

We ask for 24-hour notice for cancellations. Late cancellations may incur fees. Please call to cancel.

Do you have a dress code?

Business casual is recommended. We maintain an upscale, sophisticated atmosphere.

Can I bring my own wine?

Outside beverages are not permitted. We offer an excellent wine selection and craft beverages.
